A theological boundary

Direct My Steps can help you find Scripture, pray and reflect. It does not speak for God. App reflection is labeled separately from Scripture and every surfaced passage can be opened in context.

Why this Scripture?

Scripture vs. reflection

The verse shown is Scripture. The explanation, prayer and suggested step are app-generated guidance. Direct My Steps should never blur those two.
