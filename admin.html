Invitations

Make one link per friend. Each link works once, for one account, and expires after 30 days. Send it to that friend only.

Or use your open link: one link, no limit, for as many people as you like. Everyone who joins with it gets lifetime access and their own link for 3 more.

Shared by your friends

Every friend you invite gets their own link in the app to pass on. Each one works for up to 3 people, who also get lifetime access.
